A JOURNEY THROUGH HISTORY

Welcome to Uttar Pradesh

Uttar Pradesh is one of India's most culturally rich states. It is home to ancient cities, magnificent architecture, spiritual traditions and vibrant celebrations. Varanasi, one of its most iconic destinations, stands as a living symbol of India's timeless cultural heritage.

ARCHITECTURAL WONDERS

Historic Monuments

Discover the stories carved into the history of Uttar Pradesh.

Agra

Taj Mahal

A world-famous marble mausoleum and UNESCO World Heritage Site, built by Emperor Shah Jahan.

Varanasi

Kashi Vishwanath Temple

One of the most revered temples dedicated to Lord Shiva, located in the heart of Varanasi.

Agra

Agra Fort

A grand Mughal fortress known for its impressive red sandstone architecture and royal history.

Varanasi

Dashashwamedh Ghat

A famous riverside ghat known for its spectacular evening Ganga Aarti ceremony.

A TASTE OF TRADITION

Famous Cuisine

Discover the delicious flavours that make Uttar Pradesh special.

Breakfast

Kachori Sabzi

A popular breakfast featuring crispy kachoris served with flavourful potato curry.

Street Food

Banarasi Chaat

A delicious collection of tangy, spicy and sweet street-food specialities.

Main Course

Awadhi Biryani

Aromatic rice cooked with traditional spices, reflecting the royal cuisine of Awadh.

Dessert

Malaiyo

A delicate seasonal milk-based dessert traditionally enjoyed in Varanasi during winter.

LIVING TRADITIONS

Art & Culture

Explore the artistic heritage and cultural identity of the region.

Banarasi Silk

Varanasi is famous for its beautifully woven silk sarees, known for their detailed designs and rich craftsmanship.

Classical Music

The city has a rich musical tradition, with strong connections to Hindustani classical music and the shehnai.

Traditional Dance

Kathak, one of India's major classical dance forms, has an important historical connection with Uttar Pradesh.

Handicrafts

Woodwork, metal crafts, embroidery and traditional textiles showcase the creativity of local artisans.
